Sedation Dentistry

Sedation Dentistry in Downtown Vancouver

Atlantis Dental offers conscious sedation, or sleep dentistry, as an option for uneasy patients.

Atlantis Dental offers conscious sedation as an alternative for patients that require it.

Oral Sedation Dentistry has many benefits in helping you attain the oral health care you require and deserve. It is an excellent way to combat anxiety, to deal with strong gag reflexes, or to permit the completion of multiple treatments in a single extended visit.

We can offer Oral Sedation so that you wake up feeling refreshed and with a beautiful, healthy new smile.

We can also offer Nitrous sedation at our Cambie office, by appointment only.

Contact Atlantis Dental for more information about Sleep Dentistry today.
