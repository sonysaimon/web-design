Signs it's time to call

  • Oil spots under the car
  • Overheating or a sweet coolant smell
  • A slipping clutch or a high, spongy pedal
  • Grinding when you shift
  • Whining or humming from the back of the car
  • Knocking, ticking or smoke from the engine

What we do

Engine repair

Leaks, gaskets, cooling systems, timing components and running problems, diagnosed properly first.

Clutches

Clutch kits, flywheels, hydraulics and cables for manual cars and trucks.

Rear ends & drivetrain

Differentials, axles, CV joints and driveshafts, the noises that start at the back of the car.
