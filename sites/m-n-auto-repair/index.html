How it works

No work without your OK.

The thing customers mention most after “honest” is that we explain. Here's what happens when you bring your car in.

  1. Call or drop by. Tell us what the car is doing. Breakdown? BCAA can tow it straight to us.
  2. We find the real problem. Then we call you with the options and the price, and what can safely wait.
  3. You decide. Nothing gets fixed until you say yes. Take time to think about it if you need to.
  4. Pick it up. Many jobs are done the same or next day. We'll call as soon as it's ready.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else, call (604) 872-2211.

Do I need an appointment?

Call ahead and we'll tell you when we can look at it. For a breakdown, BCAA can tow your car straight to us at 530 E Broadway.

Will you tell me the price before you start?

Yes. We look at the car first, call you with what it needs and what it costs, and only go ahead once you say so.

Do you work on my make?

We service all makes and models: Japanese, domestic and European, new and old.

Do you still do AirCare?

AirCare ended in 2014, so BC cars don't need emissions tests any more. We still do out-of-province and pre-purchase inspections.

Do you work on propane or natural-gas vehicles?

Yes, we're propane and natural-gas certified.

Where do I park?

Pull into the lot in front of the blue and teal building with the yellow stripe on East Broadway.
