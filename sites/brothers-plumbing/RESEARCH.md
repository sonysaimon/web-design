## Design plan
Subject: 50-year west-end Toronto family plumbing firm; audience: owners of pre-war brick houses in the Junction / Davenport / High Park belt (clay laterals, lead service lines, cast-iron stacks, basement backups). Page job: get a call or a booking.
Palette: navy #0F2440 (base, from logo) · navy-deep #091A30 · copper #B8622B (pipe) · copper-bright #D9793A (hover/stamp) · porcelain #F2F4F6 (fixture white, cool not cream) · white #FFFFFF · ink #14202E · line rgba(15,36,64,.14)
Type: Barlow Condensed 600/700 (van-lettering condensed display, sentence case) · IBM Plex Sans 400/600 body · IBM Plex Mono 500 for eyebrows, work-order labels, prices (ledger feel).
Signature: the "work order" — a dated service-slip card in the hero built from a real review (job, tech, neighbourhood, time to arrival, price-before-work line) with a copper SINCE 1969 stamp; the same slip format carries reviews and project captions across the site.
Hero interaction: "What's going on?" symptom chips (clogged drain, toilet, leak, no hot water, basement water, sewer smell, frozen pipe) that route to the matching service page and prefill the booking form.
Structure (multi-page lean): index · plumbing (hub) · drains-sewer (hub) · emergency · about (story, crew names, reviews) · service-areas (map + grouped neighbourhoods) · book (form, hours, map) · services/<slug> × ~18 from one data file · generated by build.py from src/ partials.

Hours decision: "Open 7 days, 7 am – 7 pm · emergency line answered 24/7" (site says 7–7 and that calls are answered at 3 am; Google says open 24 hours).
