What's different about west-end plumbing

Most houses between High Park and Dufferin went up between 1900 and 1940. That means galvanized or lead water services, clay drain tiles, cast-iron stacks, and basements that were never meant to be finished. We have been opening those walls and floors since 1969, so we know what is behind them before we start.

Two things the City of Toronto will help pay for: a mainline backwater valve and a sump pump, through the Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy. If you have ever had water in the basement, start with those two pages.
