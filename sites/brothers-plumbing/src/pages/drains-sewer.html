Clear it, or fix it?

A first clog gets cleared. A clog that comes back in the same drain gets a camera, because something in the pipe is catching debris: a belly holding water, roots at a joint, or a cracked clay tile. We show you the video and give you two prices, one to keep clearing it and one to repair it, so the decision is yours and not a surprise.

If sewage has ever come up a floor drain, the answer is a mainline backwater valve. The City of Toronto subsidizes it. We install it, pull the permit, and help with the paperwork.
