Two City of Toronto programs worth knowing

Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy. The City pays a portion of the installed cost of a mainline backwater valve, a sump pump, and disconnecting your downspouts or weeping tile from the sewer. We install, pull the permit, and help with the application.

Lead service line replacement. Toronto Water replaces the public side of a lead water service on request. We replace the private side from the property line into the house and coordinate so both halves are done at once.
