Lead pipe replacement

Pre-1950s houses still have lead water services. We replace the private side to the main.

Call 647-247-2230 Book online
Photo slotReal lead pipe replacement job photo from the owner goes here

The problem

Many houses in the Junction, Davenport, Parkdale and the Annex were built before 1950 and still have a lead service line bringing water in from the street. The City replaces its side; the private side from the property line to the house is the owner's job.

How we fix it

We locate the service, trench or pull a new copper line to the City connection, and coordinate with Toronto Water for the public side so the whole run is done at once.

What to expect

Usually a one-day job. Water is off for a few hours. We restore the lawn or walkway opening.

Questions

Before you call

How do I know if my service is lead?

A dull grey pipe coming through the basement wall that scratches to shiny silver is lead. We can confirm in minutes.

Is there a City program?

Toronto Water replaces the public side on request and offers a priority program. We will tell you how to register.
