Pipe replacement and repiping

Galvanized supply lines, old waste pipes and failing drains replaced with as little disruption as possible.

Call (604) 312-2686Request a visit

Sounds familiar? Rusty water, low pressure, repeated leaks, or a collapsed waste line.

PlumbingDrainsRepairsRenovationsInstallationsSame-day hot water tanks

Galvanized steel pipe from the 1920s to 1950s rusts from the inside until pressure drops and water runs brown. Replacing it with copper or PEX fixes both for good. We plan the run to use existing openings wherever we can.

Waste and drain pipes fail differently: a cracked cast-iron stack or a collapsed line under the basement floor. We find the problem and look for the solution that avoids unnecessary demolition, which in one Dunbar house meant rerouting around the slab instead of cutting through it.

What's included

  • Whole-house and partial repiping in copper or PEX
  • Replacement of galvanized and polybutylene supply lines
  • Waste stack and drain line replacement
  • New shut-off valves at every fixture
  • Patching coordinated with your drywaller or contractor

In West Side homes

If your house was built before 1960 and still has original supply pipe, a repipe is the single biggest plumbing upgrade you can make. We can look at it during any service call and tell you what you have.

Questions about repiping

How long does a repipe take?

A typical two-bathroom house takes a few days of plumbing work, and the water is only off for part of each day. We give you a schedule before we start.

Will you have to open a lot of walls?

Some, but far fewer than people expect. We use the basement, closets and existing chases wherever possible.
