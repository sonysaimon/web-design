Toilet repairs and replacement

Repairs the same day where we can; a new toilet installed when the old one has had its time.

Call (604) 312-2686Request a visit

Sounds familiar? Running, rocking, slow to flush or leaking at the base.

PlumbingDrainsRepairsRenovationsInstallationsSame-day hot water tanks

A toilet that runs between flushes can waste hundreds of litres a day. Most of the time it is a flapper, fill valve or flush valve, and we carry the parts to fix it on the spot.

When a toilet is cracked, leaking at the floor or simply thirty years old, we supply and install a new one, reseal the flange and make sure it sits solid.

What's included

  • Running, weak-flush and non-flushing toilets
  • Wax seal and flange repairs
  • Supply line and shut-off valve replacement
  • Supply and installation of new toilets
  • Removal and disposal of the old unit

In West Side homes

Plenty of houses on the West Side still have 1990s toilets. A modern unit uses a fraction of the water and flushes better, and we can install it in a single visit.

Questions about toilets

Can you bring a new toilet with you?

Yes. Tell us roughly what you have and what you want when you call, and we will arrive with a suitable unit on the truck.

My toilet is blocked. Is that a toilet job or a drain job?

Either way we handle it. If the blockage is past the toilet we clear the line.
