Faucets, taps and fixtures

Kitchen and bathroom faucets, laundry taps, shower valves and outdoor hose bibs, repaired or replaced.

Call (604) 312-2686Request a visit

Sounds familiar? Dripping tap, stiff handle, a shower that will not hold its temperature.

PlumbingDrainsRepairsRenovationsInstallationsSame-day hot water tanks

A dripping tap is the most common call we get. Some are a cartridge or washer away from fixed; others are worn through and better replaced. We carry common cartridges and can bring a new faucet to fit your sink.

We also replace the taps people forget about: the laundry taps that have not turned in years, the hose bib that split in a cold snap, and the shower valve that scalds when someone flushes.

What's included

  • Kitchen and bathroom faucet repair and replacement
  • Laundry taps and washing machine shut-offs
  • Shower and tub valves, including pressure-balanced valves
  • Frost-free outdoor hose bibs
  • Installation of fixtures you have already bought

In West Side homes

Original 1920s laundry taps are still turning in a lot of Dunbar basements. When they finally give up, we replace them with proper quarter-turn valves so the washer can be shut off easily.

Questions about faucets

Can I buy my own faucet and have you install it?

Of course. Have it on site and we will fit it. We are happy to recommend brands that hold up.

Do you fix shower valves inside the wall?

Yes. Most can be rebuilt or replaced through the existing opening without new tile.
