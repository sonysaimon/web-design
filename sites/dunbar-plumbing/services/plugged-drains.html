Plugged and slow drains

Kitchen sinks, tubs, showers, laundry and floor drains cleared, usually the same day you call.

Call (604) 312-2686Request a visit

Sounds familiar? Sink gurgles, tub drains slowly, water backs up.

PlumbingDrainsRepairsRenovationsInstallationsSame-day hot water tanks

A slow drain rarely fixes itself. Grease, hair and soap build up until one morning the sink is full. We snake the line, clear the blockage and run water to make sure it is actually gone, not just moved further down.

West Side houses add their own wrinkles: cast-iron stacks from the 1920s, clay drain tile outside, and decades of kitchen renovations that left odd joints behind. We have cleared drains in these houses for over twenty years and carry the right cable and cutters on the truck.

What's included

  • Kitchen, bathroom and laundry sink drains
  • Tub, shower and floor drains
  • Toilet and main line blockages
  • Garburator and dishwasher drain lines
  • Clean-up of the work area before we leave

In West Side homes

Many Dunbar, Point Grey and Kitsilano houses still drain through original cast iron and clay tile. If a line keeps blocking, we will tell you honestly whether it needs clearing again or a section replaced.

Questions about drains

How quickly can you clear a blocked drain?

Call before noon on a weekday and we can usually be there the same day. Our hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Do you charge extra for driving out?

No. We are based on the West Side and there are no travel time charges.

Should I use a chemical drain cleaner first?

We would rather you did not. Caustic cleaners can damage old pipes and make the job harder and less safe. Run hot water, and give us a call.
