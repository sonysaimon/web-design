Leaks and pipe repairs

Find the leak, fix it properly, and leave the house the way we found it.

Call (604) 312-2686Request a visit

Sounds familiar? A drip under the sink, a damp ceiling, a pinhole in a copper line.

PlumbingDrainsRepairsRenovationsInstallationsSame-day hot water tanks

Leaks show up as a stain on a ceiling, a cupboard that smells damp, or a water bill that jumped. We trace the source, open only what needs opening and repair the pipe, joint or valve that failed.

Older West Side homes often mix original galvanized steel with later copper and plastic. Those transitions are where leaks like to start. We carry fittings for all three so the repair happens on the first visit.

What's included

  • Dripping and burst pipe repairs
  • Leaking shut-off valves and supply lines
  • Shower, tub and tap leaks behind the wall
  • Leaking drain and waste connections
  • Advice on whether a section should be replaced instead

In West Side homes

If water is pouring out right now, close the main shut-off (usually in the basement where the water line enters, or at the front of the house) and then call us.

Questions about leaks

Can you find a leak without tearing out the wall?

Usually. We start with the fixture and the fittings closest to the damp area and open the wall only when there is no other way.

Do you repair old galvanized pipe?

Yes, and we will tell you plainly when a repair is the right call and when the section is too far gone and should be replaced.

What if it is after hours?

Shut off the water, then call or text us. We return messages first thing in the morning.
