The salon

Concrete, warm wood, and a V in white neon.

VSalon sits on the ground floor of the MC2 towers on SW Marine Drive. Inside, a curved wall of wooden slats wraps the chairs, the front wall is bare concrete with the V glowing on it, and a bear keeps watch at the desk.

The team specialises in balayage and colour technique, and only works with luxury hair product lines, so your hair leaves healthier than it arrived. When you walk out, you should feel as beautiful on the outside as you are within.

Good to know

Before you come in.

Do I need an appointment?
Yes. Book on Fresha for instant confirmation, or call (604) 834‑4930. Messages sent through Facebook Messenger are not checked.
How is colour priced?
Colour, balayage, toner and root work are quoted at your consultation, because the price depends on your length, hair history and the result you want. Book a Consultation 咨询 first if you'd like a quote before committing.
What if I'm not happy?
Redo appointments are listed on Fresha at no charge. Get in touch and the team will book you back in.
Can I book with a specific stylist?
Yes. Every service is offered by David, Goldie, Andy and Yi Yang; pick a name when you book, or "any professional" for the earliest slot.
Where do I park?
Free street parking and a free parking garage nearby, plus paid garage parking. The Canada Line's Marine Drive station is across the street.
How do I pay?
Credit, debit and tap. You can also reach the salon on WeChat: Vsalonbeauty.
