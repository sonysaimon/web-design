What we do

Everything a family needs, under one roof.

From a child’s first check-up to implants and Invisalign, Dr. Lam does the work himself and explains the options before anything starts. No prices on the website: every mouth is different, and you get a written plan before treatment.

Children

The dentist your kids will not dread.

Before opening on Broadway, Dr. Lam spent more than ten years at a non-profit community dental clinic for kids. He knows how to talk a six-year-old through a filling, and when to stop and try again next time. Families in our reviews have been bringing their children since 2012; some of those kids are now in university.
