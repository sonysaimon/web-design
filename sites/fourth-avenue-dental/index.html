New patients

Your first visit, without the guesswork.

Before you come

Bring your medical history, a list of medications and your insurance details. If you want to fill in forms at home, ask the desk to send them ahead.

At the appointment

A first visit is longer on purpose. You meet your dentist, and the exam maps your teeth and facial structure so the plan is built around you.

Paying for care

The front desk helps with insurance claims. If a treatment is more than you planned for, ask about payment arrangements before anything starts.

Tooth pain or an accident

Call (604) 736-2505. A knocked-out tooth can often be saved if you rinse it (no scrubbing), keep it in your cheek or in milk, and get here quickly.

How we work

Map first. Then plan.

The clinic’s approach starts with the whole face, not one tooth. A new patient exam records your dental and facial structure, and the plan comes from that picture rather than from a list of procedures. Most care, including orthodontics and implants, happens in-house.

  • Exam first, plan second, treatment only once you have seen both
  • Modern equipment; patients mention how quick the x-rays are
  • Gentle with sensitive patients and nervous ones
  • Hygiene visits on a schedule that fits you, with a reminder call

A craft most clinics have dropped

Still placing 24-carat gold foil.

Gold foil fillings were the standard before silver amalgam, and many placed in the 1950s are still in service. By the clinic’s own count it is one of fewer than 250 practices in the world that still do them, and it hosts a gold foil study club for BC dentists. Ask for gold if you want a filling built to outlast you.
