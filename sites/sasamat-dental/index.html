Services

From a cleaning to a bone graft, under one roof.

General, orthodontic, surgical and cosmetic dentistry at one address. Every treatment starts with the options and their trade-offs explained in plain words, in English or Mandarin. No prices are listed here: ask us for a written estimate before any work begins.

New patients

Your first visit, without the guesswork.

What happens
A full exam, X-rays where needed, a cleaning with our hygienist if you want one the same day, and a clear plan for anything that needs doing. Nothing starts until you understand it.
What to bring
Photo ID, your dental plan details if you have one, and a list of medications. The front desk goes through your coverage with you before treatment.
