      New patients

      Bring your plan card. We do the rest.

      Before you come

      • Arrive 5 to 10 minutes early for the paperwork
      • Your extended health card, or the plan name and member number
      • ICBC claim number if this is a car-accident injury
      • For orthotics: the shoes you wear most
      • Comfortable clothes you can move in

      Paying

      • Cash, debit, Visa and Mastercard
      • Chiropractic billed directly to your extended plan, free, since 2014
      • Massage paid at the visit, with an official receipt for your insurer
      • MSP premium assistance, DVA and RCMP plans accepted
      • 24 hours’ notice to cancel, or the full visit fee applies

      After a car accident

      An approved ICBC claim covers your chiropractic here: the first visit and single-region follow-ups cost $0, two or more regions $10. Bring the claim number. Over 12 weeks since the accident? Call first so we can get pre-approval.
