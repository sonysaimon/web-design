What counts

Call right away for any of these

  • Burst or frozen pipe, or water you cannot shut off
  • Sewer backing up into a basement drain, tub or toilet
  • No water at all, or no hot water in winter
  • Toilet overflowing and it is the only washroom
  • Water heater leaking from the tank, or a gas smell near it (leave the house and call the gas utility first)
  • Commercial: a washroom or kitchen you cannot open without

Before we arrive

Three things that limit the damage

  1. 1

    Shut the water off

    Fixture valve under the sink or behind the toilet; main shut-off where the line enters the house, usually in the basement near the front wall.

  2. 2

    Kill power near the water

    If water is near outlets, a panel or the water heater, switch off that breaker.

  3. 3

    Clear the area and call

    Move what you can, put towels down, and call. We will give you an arrival window and stay on the phone if you need us to.

Questions

What should I do before you arrive?

Shut off the water. For a fixture, the small valve under the sink or behind the toilet. For a burst pipe, the main shut-off, usually where the water line enters the basement. Then move anything that can be damaged and call us.

Is there an extra charge at night?

No. The $150 visit and the flat quote are the same at 2 a.m. as at 2 p.m.

How fast can you get here?

It depends on where you are and what is already booked. We tell you a real window on the phone, and we call if it changes.

Is a slow drain an emergency?

Not usually. Water you cannot stop, sewage coming up, no water at all, or no hot water in winter are the ones to call about immediately. Everything else we book for the next slot.
