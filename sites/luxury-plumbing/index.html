Questions

Before you call

How much does a visit cost?

A diagnostic visit is $150. If you go ahead with the repair, that $150 comes off the bill. You get a flat quote before any work starts.

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. Call (647) 560-2948 at any hour. Nights and weekends are the same rate as weekdays.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based at 230 Mavety St in The Junction and serve the west end and downtown Toronto: Bloor West Village, High Park, Roncesvalles, Parkdale, Liberty Village and the core. Elsewhere in the GTA by request.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our work is done by licensed plumbers and the company carries liability insurance. Ask for the licence number when you call.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. Restaurants, shops, offices and small buildings, scheduled around your hours, including overnight.

Will you clean up afterwards?

Always. Clean work is the whole point: drop sheets down, debris out, cabinet wiped.
