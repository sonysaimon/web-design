Subject: a small, new, tidy-work plumbing crew in The Junction serving west-end + downtown Toronto. Audience: homeowners/condo owners with an urgent problem or a bathroom/kitchen upgrade. Page's single job: get a tap on Call.
Tokens: ink #15171A (warm charcoal base) · slate #22262B (cards) · brass #C9A14A (accent, from the tub fixtures) · brass-light #E4C978 · porcelain #F2EEE6 (light sections/text) · tile #9AA0A6 (muted) · oxblood #8B2E2E (emergency marker only).
Type: Bodoni Moda (display, 500–600, tight) · Work Sans (body 400/500/600) · IBM Plex Mono (eyebrows, ticket data, uppercase letterspaced) — the work-order/invoice vernacular.
Signature: the job ticket. Hero proof card = finished shower photo with a perforated ticket strip (job type, scope, Toronto, 5.0 stamp). "Recent work" = tickets; before/after = one ticket, two photos. Portrait phone photos fit the ticket format natively.
Avoided defaults: no pure-black + neon; warm charcoal alternating with porcelain sections; serif display not Playfair/Fraunces/Instrument; no icon grid for services (photo cards + problem lines); numbered steps only in "How a visit works" (a real sequence).
Structure: Home + /services/<slug>.html ×6 + /emergency.html, one template. Nav: Services · Emergency · Our work · Reviews · Areas · [Call] [Request a visit].
