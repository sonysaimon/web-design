Questions

Asked about emergency plumbing, 24/7

Do you charge extra at night or on weekends?

We pledge not to overcharge for emergency work. You get the price before we start, whatever the hour.

How fast can you get here?

We aim for same-day on every emergency call and often faster; one Burnaby customer had a technician at the door within the hour for a failed hot water tank.

What should I do before you arrive?

Shut the main water valve (usually where the water line enters the house, near the meter or hot water tank), turn off power to any flooded area, and move what you can. Call us and we'll walk you through it.

Do you take strata emergencies?

Yes. Strata and property management accounts get 24-hour priority service, including flooded parkades, gardens and in-suite emergencies.
