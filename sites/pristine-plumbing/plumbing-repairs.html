Repairs & fixtures · Burnaby & the Lower Mainland

Small jobs done right, the first time

Everyday plumbing repairs and fixture replacements in Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam and Vancouver homes.

Call 778-980-1883Request a visit

Most of our days are the jobs that keep a house working: a faucet that won't stop dripping, a toilet that runs, a garburator that hums and quits, a hose bib that leaks every spring, a shut-off valve that doesn't shut off anymore. We carry the common parts on the van so most repairs are finished in one visit.

We'll tell you honestly when a repair is the right call and when a replacement will save you money. If something's an easy fix you can do yourself, we'll say so. Reviews mention that honesty more than anything else.

Every service call includes a free home plumbing inspection. While we're there we look over your shut-offs, supply lines, water heater and visible pipework and tell you what's fine and what to keep an eye on.

Questions

Asked about plumbing repairs & fixtures

Do you give a price before you start?

Always. The technician diagnoses the problem, gives you the price, and only starts when you say go.

Can you fix it in one visit?

Usually. Our vans carry the common faucets, valves, supply lines and toilet parts. Special-order fixtures take a second visit.

What's the free home plumbing inspection?

On every service call we look over your visible plumbing, shut-offs and water heater and tell you about anything that could become a problem. No charge, no pressure.

Do you install fixtures I bought myself?

Yes. Bring the faucet, toilet or sink you've chosen and we'll install it properly.
