Hot water · Burnaby & the Lower Mainland

Hot water back, installed to code

Hot water tank repairs, replacements and upgrades across Burnaby, the Tri-Cities, Vancouver and Bowen Island.

Call 778-980-1883Request a visit

A tank that leaks, rumbles or runs cold is costing you money before it fails for good. We troubleshoot first: burners, elements, thermostats, drain valves and relief valves can all be repaired, and we'll tell you when a repair is worth it and when the tank is at the end of its life.

When it's time to replace, we install to current code: drip pan, thermal expansion tank, seismic strapping and correct piping for the temperature and pressure relief valve. If space allows we can step you up to a larger tank. The old tank goes with us.

Burnaby, the Tri-Cities and Vancouver are our daily routes, and yes, we take the ferry to Bowen Island.

What's included

  • Diagnosis and repair of gas and electric tanks
  • Tank replacement with removal of the old unit
  • Drip pan, expansion tank and seismic strapping
  • Relief valve piping brought to code
  • Upsizing where space permits
  • Shut-off and supply line replacement

Questions

Asked about hot water tank repair & replacement

Repair or replace?

If the tank itself is leaking, replace. If it's a valve, element, thermostat or burner, repair is usually worthwhile. We'll quote both when both are options.

How long does a replacement take?

Most straight swaps are done the same day, often in a few hours.

Do you install tankless?

Ask us about your home. Our everyday work is tank repair and replacement; we'll tell you honestly whether tankless makes sense for your layout.

Will the install pass inspection?

Yes. Pan, expansion tank, strapping and relief piping are all done to code on every install.
