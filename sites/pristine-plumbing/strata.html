Strata & property management · Burnaby & the Lower Mainland

One plumbing partner for the whole building

Strata councils and property managers across the Lower Mainland call us for in-suite repairs, building drainage and 24-hour emergencies.

Call 778-980-1883Request a visit

Managing a building means plumbing problems arrive in bulk: a leak in one suite that shows up in the one below, a parkade drain that backs up every November, garden drains that flood the walkway. We work for strata corporations and property management companies across the Lower Mainland, from four-unit townhouses to large complexes.

Corporate accounts get 24-hour priority emergency service and a team that already knows your building. We document every visit with photos and a written report so council and owners see exactly what was found and what was done.

Our strata work is reviewed on HomeStars and Google, including a four-unit townhouse where we assessed and replaced failed sump pumps and drainage, and we're happy to put you in touch with a council we work with.

What's included

  • In-suite fixture repairs, installs and replacements
  • Unit maintenance inspections
  • 24-hour priority emergency service for accounts
  • Flooding in basements, underground parkades, gardens and lawns
  • Building drainage camera inspections and hydro flushing
  • Drainage system repair and installation
  • Photo-documented reports for council

Price before work starts. Free home plumbing inspection with every call.

Questions

Asked about strata & property management plumbing

Do you take on new strata accounts?

Yes. Call or send a request with the building address and what you're dealing with, and we'll set up a site visit.

How fast do you respond to a building emergency?

Accounts get 24-hour priority. A parkade flood or a suite leak is attended the same day.

Can you invoice the strata and the owner separately?

Yes. We're used to splitting in-suite and common-property work on the paperwork.

Do you provide reports for council?

Every visit is documented with photos and a written summary suitable for council minutes and insurance.
