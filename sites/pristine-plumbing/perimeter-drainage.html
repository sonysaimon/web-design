Perimeter drainage & sumps · Burnaby & the Lower Mainland

Keep the rain out of your basement

Camera inspections, hydro flushing, sump clean-outs and full perimeter drainage replacements, with City permits, in Burnaby, Coquitlam, New Westminster and Vancouver.

Call 778-980-1883Request a visit

Every house with a basement or crawlspace has a perimeter drainage system: the pipe that runs around the footing, catches the roof water from the rain leaders and the ground water around the foundation, and carries it to the City storm connection. When it plugs with sediment, pine needles or roots, the water finds its way inside. That's the flooded basement, the efflorescence on the wall, the musty smell.

Systems that have never been cleaned are the ones that fail. We camera the whole run, show you what's in it, and clean it out with hydro flushing and snaking. For most homes that's every two to three years. If a section has collapsed or the original clay tile is past saving, we repair it or replace the system with a proper two-pipe design: one line for roof water, one for ground water, both tied to a new sump and out to the City storm main.

We use a vacuum truck when a dig is next to a tree or a driveway you'd rather keep, obtain the City permits, pass the inspections and backfill with a stone slinger so your yard goes back together.

What's included

  • Full camera inspection of the perimeter system with a report
  • Hydro flushing and snaking of drain tile and outlet lines
  • Sump chamber clean-out, risers and new lids
  • Repair of broken or collapsed sections
  • Two-pipe perimeter drainage replacement with new sump
  • New-construction perimeter drainage installation
  • Vacuum-truck excavation and slinger backfill
  • City permits and inspections

Questions

Asked about perimeter drainage & sump systems

How often should perimeter drains be cleaned?

Every two to three years depending on the age and material of the system and how many trees are nearby. If it has never been done, book an inspection before the fall rains.

Can you tell if my system is failing before it floods?

Yes. A camera inspection shows sediment levels, root intrusion, cracks and dips. You'll get the footage and a plain-language recommendation.

Do I need a permit for perimeter drain replacement?

Yes, and we take care of it. Every system we install is permitted and inspected by the City.

Will you wreck my garden?

We plan the dig around what you care about. For tight spots and tree roots we bring in a vacuum truck, which digs with suction instead of a bucket.
