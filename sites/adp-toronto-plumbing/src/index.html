How it works

From the call to the lawn going back.

A sewer job is the one time most people have a crew digging up their yard. Here's the order it happens in.

  1. Call or send the form

    Emergencies get a plumber dispatched. Everything else gets a visit booked at a time that suits you.

  2. Camera first, then a flat-rate quote

    CCTV inspection with video and sound shows exactly where the problem is. You see the footage and the price before anything starts. The estimate is free.

  3. Permits and City paperwork

    We pull the plumbing permit, book the inspection and, where it applies, file your Toronto subsidy application.

  4. The work

    Trenchless where it saves you money, excavation where it doesn't. The crew protects the neighbour's side yard and puts your lawn back.

  5. Inspection passed, warranty on

    The City signs off, you get the paperwork, and your drain work is covered for 25 years.

Do you charge for an estimate?

How quickly can you come?
We're open 24 hours. Emergencies get the next available crew; for everything else we give you a time when you call {{PHONE}}.
What does the Toronto basement flooding subsidy cover?
As of May 1, 2026 the City of Toronto pays 80% of the invoiced cost up to $1,600 per backwater valve (two maximum), $2,250 for a sump pump, $300 for a battery backup, $400 for weeping-tile severance and capping, and $500 for a home plumbing assessment, to a maximum of $6,650 per property. We file the application for you. Details and the calculator.
Do you handle permits and City inspections?
Yes. We pull the plumbing permit, book the City inspection and deal with the inspector on site. Several of our reviews mention this specifically.
What warranty do you offer?
A 25-year warranty on all completed drain work, and we guarantee our workmanship on every job. We fix it right the first time.
Do you work outside Toronto?
Yes, across the GTA: Toronto, North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Mississauga, Brampton, Caledon, Oakville, Burlington, Milton and Halton Hills. Service areas.
