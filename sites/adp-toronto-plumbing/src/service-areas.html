Service areas

Where the five trucks go.

We dispatch from 51 Homewood Avenue in downtown Toronto and cover the GTA. Below, what's different about the pipes and the rebates in each area, so you know what to expect before we arrive.

One number for all of it

Whether you're in Cabbagetown or Caledon, you call {{PHONE}}, you get a flat-rate quote before any work starts, and the same crews do the job. Response is fastest inside Toronto, where most of our work is; for the outer GTA we book a time when you call.

Subsidies are municipal, so they change at the city line. Toronto's program is the big one. Vaughan has run its own backwater-valve subsidy; ask us for the current terms when you call.

City of Toronto

Toronto

  • Downtown & Cabbagetown
  • East York & the Danforth
  • North York
  • Scarborough
  • Etobicoke
  • York & Weston

Pre-war houses in the old city sit on clay sewer laterals that tree roots find eventually, and many homes built before the mid-1950s still have a lead water service. Those are the two jobs we do most: sewer replacement (trenchless where the pipe allows it) and a new copper service to the City shutoff. Post-war bungalows in Scarborough, North York and Etobicoke have longer laterals and more sump pumps.

Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy applies: up to $6,650 per property. We file it.

York Region

Vaughan, Woodbridge, Richmond Hill, Markham

  • Vaughan
  • Woodbridge
  • Richmond Hill
  • Markham

Newer subdivisions mostly, with PVC laterals and sump pumps that get tested only when the spring thaw arrives. We replace pumps, add battery backups and install mainline backwater valves. Older Woodbridge and Thornhill streets bring the same clay-pipe problems as Toronto.

Vaughan has offered a backwater-valve subsidy of its own; ask us for current terms.

Peel Region

Mississauga, Brampton, Caledon

  • Mississauga
  • Brampton
  • Caledon

A mix of 1960s–80s housing in Mississauga and Brampton, where water services and drains are reaching the end of their first life, and rural Caledon properties where the job is often a water service, sump system or a long run of drain. Commercial and multi-unit work in Mississauga is a regular call.

Halton Region

Oakville, Burlington, Milton, Halton Hills

  • Oakville
  • Burlington
  • Milton
  • Halton Hills, Georgetown & Acton

Our furthest regular area. Drain cleaning, camera inspections, sump pumps and waterproofing in Oakville and Burlington's older lakeshore streets; water services and drain installation for new builds and additions in Milton and Georgetown.
