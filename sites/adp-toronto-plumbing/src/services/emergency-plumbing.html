While you wait for us

  • Burst or leaking pipe: close the main water shutoff. It's usually at the front of the basement where the service pipe comes in, before the meter.
  • Sewer backup: stop running water anywhere in the house. Every flush and every load of laundry goes to the same place.
  • Sewage on the floor: keep people and pets out of it. Move what you can up off the floor.
  • Frozen pipe: open the tap it feeds, and warm the pipe gently if you can reach it. Never with a torch.
  • Water in the street or on the lawn: that may be the water service. Call us and the City.

What happens when you call

The phone is answered around the clock. We ask what's happening, where you are and whether the water is shut off, and dispatch the nearest crew. The plumber arrives with a stocked truck, finds the cause, and gives you a flat-rate price for the fix before starting. If it's a bigger job, say a collapsed sewer, we'll make it safe tonight and quote the full repair for daylight, with a camera inspection so you can see what you're paying for.

Our reviews mention a Sunday-night visit with a newborn in the house, a same-day call-back for a business with leaks, a 2-hour arrival. We don't promise a number of minutes we can't keep in Toronto traffic; we promise that the phone is answered and a crew is coming.

Questions

Is there an extra charge at night or on weekends?
You get a flat-rate quote before any work starts, whatever the hour, so there are no surprises on the invoice. Ask when you call and we'll tell you the price for your job.
What counts as an emergency?
Anything that is damaging the house or stops you living in it: a burst or leaking pipe you can't shut off, sewage backing up, a toilet that won't stop overflowing, no water at all, a frozen pipe, a water main leak.
Do you handle the sewage cleanup?
We clear the cause and clean up our own work area. For a basement that has flooded with sewage we'll tell you honestly whether it needs a restoration company as well.

Find your main shutoff

  • Front wall of the basement, where the pipe enters
  • Before the water meter
  • Round wheel or lever handle
  • Turn clockwise to close
  • If it won't close, the City's shutoff is at the curb: call 311
