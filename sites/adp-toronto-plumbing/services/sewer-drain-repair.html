Sewer backup and drain repair.

Sewage in the basement, a gurgling floor drain, the same clog coming back every spring. In Toronto that usually means tree roots in a clay lateral. We put a camera down it, show you the footage, and give you a flat-rate price to fix it for good.

Why Toronto sewers back up

Most houses in the old city still drain through the clay lateral that was laid when they were built. Clay pipe comes in short sections, and every joint is a place roots can get in. Once they do, they thicken every year until a toilet flush has nowhere to go. Add a bellied section where the ground has settled, or a building trap that has collapsed, and you get a backup that snaking only postpones.

The other cause is the City's sewer surcharging during a heavy storm and pushing back up your lateral. That one is a backwater valve problem, not a pipe problem, and the City pays most of the cost to fix it.

How we fix it

Every repair starts with a CCTV inspection that records video and sound, so you see what we see and the quote matches what's actually down there. From there, three options:

  • Trenchless lining. A resin liner is pulled through the existing pipe and cured in place, making a new jointless pipe inside the old one. One small access pit, no trench across the lawn.
  • Pipe bursting. A new pipe is pulled through while the old one is broken outward. Replaces the whole run from a pit at each end.
  • Excavation. When a section has collapsed or bellied, we open that section, replace it, and compact and restore the ground.

We pull the permit, book the City inspection and deal with the inspector. If a backwater valve goes in during the same job, we file the Toronto subsidy for it too.

Questions

Trenchless or dig: which will it be?
The camera decides. If the pipe is cracked or root-infested but still holds its shape, we line it or burst it from a small access pit. If it has collapsed or bellied, that section is excavated and replaced.
Will you dig up my whole front yard?
Usually not. Trenchless methods need one or two small pits. When excavation is needed we open only the failed section, protect the neighbour's side yard, and put the lawn back when we're done.
Is sewer repair covered by the Toronto subsidy?
The sewer lateral itself is not, but a backwater valve installed as part of the job is, up to $1,600 per device. We file that application for you.
What warranty comes with it?
All completed drain work carries a 25-year warranty on our workmanship.
