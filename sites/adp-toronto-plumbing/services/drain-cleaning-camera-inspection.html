Drain cleaning and camera inspection.

A slow kitchen sink, a tub that fills while you shower, a floor drain that burps when the washing machine empties. We clear it, and when it's the main line we put a camera down afterwards so you know whether it will be back.

Clear it, then look

Most drain companies snake the line and leave. We do that too, for the bathroom sink that's full of hair. But a main sewer line that has backed up once will usually back up again, because the cause is roots, grease, scale or a sag in the pipe, and a snake only opens a hole through it.

So for main lines and floor drains we clear the blockage and then run a CCTV camera through the pipe. You see the pipe wall, the joints and the roots on a screen, with distance markers, and the recording is yours. If the pipe is fine, you've bought peace of mind. If it isn't, you get a flat-rate quote for the repair with the evidence in hand.

The tools

  • Drain snaking. Cable machines sized for everything from a bathroom sink to a 6-inch main.
  • Hydro-jetting. High-pressure water that scours grease, scale and root hair off the full pipe wall, not just a path through it. Standard for restaurants and recurring clogs.
  • CCTV inspection. Self-levelling colour cameras recording video and sound, with a locator so we can mark exactly where a problem is from the surface.
  • Pipe tracing. Find where a lateral actually runs before anyone digs.

Questions

Snaking or hydro-jetting?
Snaking cuts through a blockage and is right for hair, a toy, a single root ball. Hydro-jetting scours the whole pipe wall with high-pressure water and is right for grease, scale and recurring root growth. The camera tells us which.
Do I need a camera inspection for a simple clog?
Not for a one-off bathroom sink. For a main line, a floor drain, or anything that has clogged more than once, yes: it's the difference between clearing the symptom and knowing the cause.
Do I get the footage?
Yes. Our cameras record video and sound, and the recording is yours, useful for insurance, a home purchase or a second opinion.
Do you inspect drains for home buyers?
Yes. A sewer camera inspection before closing on a pre-war Toronto house is one of the cheapest ways to avoid a five-figure surprise.
