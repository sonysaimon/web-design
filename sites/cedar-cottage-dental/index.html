New patients

Your first visit, without surprises.

What to bring

  • Your dental insurance details, if you have a plan
  • A list of any medications you take
  • Recent X-rays from a previous dentist, if you have them
  • Any questions. Write them down; they get answered

What happens

  • A full examination and hygiene visit
  • A conversation about your current oral health
  • Time spent on how to look after your teeth at home
  • A treatment plan explained in plain language, if you need one

Insurance, pain and hours

  • Bring your plan details and the front desk will go over coverage before treatment. Ask about direct billing when you book
  • Toothache or a broken tooth? Call (604) 684-7717 and say so; we look for the earliest opening
  • Open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm
