Root canal treatment

Your root canal, done by the dentist you already know.

Many family practices refer root canals out to a specialist. Dr. Rahman finished a master’s degree in endodontics, the specialty of root canal treatment, in 2012, and worked as an endodontist as well as a general dentist before opening Lotus Dental. So the tooth that is keeping you up at night is treated here, by her, usually with the same gentle anaesthetic patients keep mentioning in their reviews.

  1. Look firstA digital x-ray and an honest read on whether the tooth can be saved.
  2. TreatThe infected pulp is removed and the canals cleaned and sealed, under local anaesthetic.
  3. ProtectA filling or crown so the tooth keeps working for years.

Meet the doctor

Dr. Basant A. Rahman

Born and raised in Alexandria, Egypt, Dr. Rahman earned her Bachelor of Dentistry and Oral Surgery at Alexandria University in 2000 and practised in Cairo before completing a master’s degree in endodontics in 2012. She moved to British Columbia the same year, passed the Canadian dental exams in 2013, and worked as a general dentist and endodontist in Vancouver clinics before opening Lotus Dental in Brentwood in 2016.

She has taught part-time in UBC’s Faculty of Dentistry and at Dentachieve, helping foreign-trained dentists pass their Canadian licensing exams. She is a member of the College of Dental Surgeons of BC and the BC Dental Association, and speaks English, French, German and Arabic fluently.

“In my office we have your best interest at heart. There are no limits to the energy we will expend to provide you healthy and long-lasting smiles.”

Dr. Rahman
