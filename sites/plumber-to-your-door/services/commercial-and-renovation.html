FAQ

Questions about commercial plumbing & rough-ins

Do you work after hours for businesses?

Yes. Most commercial work is scheduled around your opening hours, and the emergency line is open 24/7 for the rest.

Can you pull permits for a rough-in?

Yes. We handle the plumbing permit and book the City inspection, and we provide the drawings your contractor or designer needs.

Are you insured for commercial work?

Yes: licensed, insured and bonded, with certificates available for your property manager or condo board.
