Sump pumps, backwater valves & flood clean-up

Basement flooding protection and clean-up in Toronto

Toronto basements flood from two directions: groundwater after a storm, and sewage backing up from an overloaded city sewer. A sump pump handles the first, a backwater valve the second. If it has already happened, we pump out, dry and sanitize first, then fix the cause so you don't do this twice.

Good to know

A battery backup matters most on the night the power goes out in a storm, which is exactly when the pump is working hardest.

The City of Toronto's Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy pays up to $6,650 towards a backwater valve, a sump pump and downspout disconnection for eligible homes. We'll tell you if yours qualifies and hand you the paperwork.

FAQ

Questions about sump pumps, backwater valves & flood clean-up

My basement is flooding right now. What do I do?

Stay out of standing water if there's any chance it's near electrical. Call us: we'll walk you through the shut-offs and get a van moving.

How long does a sump pump last?

Seven to ten years for a good pump. If yours is older, runs constantly or hasn't been tested, replace it before the spring thaw rather than after.

Does insurance cover this?

Many policies cover sewer backup only if you have an endorsement, and some insurers give a discount once a backwater valve is installed. We provide the invoice and photos you'd need.
