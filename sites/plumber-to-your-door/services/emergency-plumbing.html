What we fix

On the truck and ready for

  • Burst or frozen pipes
  • Sewer backing up into the basement
  • Overflowing toilets and drains
  • Leaking or failed water heater
  • No water or no hot water
  • Water coming through a ceiling or wall
  • Flooded basement or laundry room
  • Commercial shut-downs after hours

While you wait

Shut off the main water valve. In most Toronto houses it's at the front of the basement, next to the water meter.

If the water heater is leaking, turn off its cold-water supply and the gas or breaker.

If sewage is backing up, stop running water anywhere in the house.

Move what you can off the floor. Photos help with insurance later.

FAQ

Questions about emergency plumbing, 24/7

Is there an extra charge at night or on weekends?

No. Our pricing is by the job, and the price for a job is the same at 2 am as it is at 2 pm.

What counts as an emergency?

Anything where water is actively going somewhere it shouldn't, or you have no water at all. If you're not sure, call: we'll tell you honestly whether it can wait until morning.

How fast can you get here?

In most of Toronto, Etobicoke, North York and Scarborough we aim for 60 to 90 minutes from your call. Farther out in the GTA it depends on traffic and where the nearest van is; we'll give you a real time on the phone.
