About

We're not a call centre. We're the people in the van.

Plumber To Your Door is a family-owned Toronto plumbing company started in 2021 by George, a licensed plumber with 15+ years in the trade. The business was built on the idea in the name: a plumber comes to your door, tells you the price, fixes it and stands behind it.

How we work

Three things we don't do.

  • We don't charge by the hour. You get a fixed price for the job before we start.
  • We don't charge more at night. The emergency line is open 24/7 and the price is the price.
  • We don't walk away. 12 months on parts and workmanship, 5 years on main water lines.
