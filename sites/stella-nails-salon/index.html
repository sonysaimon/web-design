Good to know

Before you come in.

Do I need to book?
No. Walk-ins are welcome every day. Booking online on Square guarantees your time and lets you pick your technician; you can also call (604) 696‑9191.
Is removal extra?
Gel manicures, pedicures and polish changes include gel removal. Removing acrylic, Gel X, dip or hard gel costs $15 to $20 on its own and $5 to $15 when you're getting another service.
Can I bring my kids?
Yes. There's a Little Ones menu for children under 10, from $25.
How long will it take?
Every price on the board shows its time. A gel manicure is 45 minutes; a full set is about an hour and a quarter to an hour and a half; combos run about 90 minutes.
How do I get there?
330 Robson St, at the base of the Eight One Nine building next to Library Square, a five-minute walk from Vancouver City Centre and Stadium–Chinatown stations.
How can I pay?
Credit, debit and tap. The salon is women-owned, wheelchair accessible at the entrance, and has a gender-neutral washroom.
