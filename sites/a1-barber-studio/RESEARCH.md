## Design plan
Subject: A1 Barber Studio, a modern four-chair men's barbershop inside Le Magasin, 332 Water St, Gastown. Audience: men 18–40 working or living downtown who want a sharp fade or scissor cut at a fair price, 7 days a week. Page job: get them to tap "Book now" (Setmore), ideally with a specific barber.

Direction: the opposite of the local competitors. They are dark, vintage, gold-on-black "gentleman cave". A1's room is bright: white plaster walls, pale oak floor, black chairs, chrome, daylight. So: a high-key, gallery-white page with black type, oak as the only warm colour, and photography doing the work. Reviews call it "clean and very modern-looking"; the site should feel like that room.

Tokens
- --paper #F7F5F0 (wall) · --white #FFFFFF · --oak #C49A6C · --oak-deep #8F6B45 · --ink #141414 (chairs, capes) · --steel #8C8C8C (chrome, secondary text) · --line rgba(20,20,20,.14)
- Display: Archivo (Google Fonts) at wdth 125 / wght 800 for headlines: wide, blunt, confident, like a shop sign but modern. Body: Archivo 400/500 at normal width. Utility: IBM Plex Mono for prices, durations, labels, hours: the precision register ("rock-steady tension, lines that pop").
- Signature: **the fade**. A smooth black-to-transparent gradient band is the one graphic device: it runs under the hero headline as a "line-up" rule, sits behind the price list header, and forms the hero backdrop behind the chair photo (ink at the top fading to paper). Nothing else decorative.
