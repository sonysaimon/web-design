Cuts & prices

Pick your cut. Book your barber.

Prices in Canadian dollars, before tax. Every cut is booked in a set time slot, so what you see is what it costs.

Not sure which one?

  • Haircut is the standard 45‑minute cut: fade or taper, blended and lined up.
  • Long / Scissor Haircut when it's past the ears or you want it shaped with scissors, not clippers.
  • Taper + Line Up is a clean‑up between full cuts, not a full haircut.
  • Add a beard to either haircut and save versus booking it separately.

Good to know

Before you come in.

When should I arrive?
Five to ten minutes before your appointment. Running late pushes everyone after you.
How do I cancel or reschedule?
Call (236) 513‑1839 up to two hours before your appointment.
Where exactly are you?
Unit 140 inside Le Magasin at 332 Water Street, Gastown. About four minutes on foot from Waterfront Station.
Can I choose my barber?
Yes. Pick Mike, Rai, Jay Jay or Leo when you book, or take the first chair free.
Do you do beards?
Yes. A beard trim is $28 on its own, or add it to a haircut for $55, or to a long scissor cut for $65.
What are the hours?
Every day, 9:00 am to 8:30 pm, including Sundays.
