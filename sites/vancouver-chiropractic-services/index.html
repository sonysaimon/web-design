      How Dr. Horowitz works

      Find the source, not just the symptom.

      Neck pain is rarely only a neck problem. Feet that roll in tilt the pelvis, the pelvis shifts the spine, and the neck pays for it at a desk eight hours a day. So the exam looks at the whole chain, from the ground up, before anything gets adjusted.

      • Gentle adjustments. Patients use that word in their reviews more than any other.
      • You leave with homework. Stretches and exercises matched to your exam, so the visit keeps working between visits.
      • Spinal hygiene. Stretching, moving, and a regular check-up stop small aches from becoming big ones.
      • Honest referrals. If chiropractic is not the right tool, he says so.
