Questions

Before you book

How much does a plumber cost in Toronto?

It depends on the job, so we do not quote blind. The technician looks, tells you the flat price, and only starts when you say yes. There are no hourly meters running. First-time customers get $40 off, and jobs over $1,000 can be financed.

How fast can you come?

Same-day service is the norm across Toronto, and emergency calls get a same-day response around the clock. You get a real arrival window when you book.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Our plumbers are licensed in Ontario and the company is fully insured. The licence number is listed in the footer of this site.

What is the warranty?

Two years on our workmanship, in writing. If something we did fails in that time, we come back and fix it.

Do you charge extra at night or on weekends?

The repair price is the same. An after-hours call-out fee applies outside regular hours and is stated before we dispatch, so there are no surprises.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Toronto: Downtown, York, North York, East York, Etobicoke and Scarborough, from our shop at 984 Caledonia Rd in North York.
