Downtown Toronto

Condos, century rowhouses, cast-iron stacks

  • In-suite leaks
  • Stack repairs
  • Lead service lines
  • Drain cameras
  • Touchless faucets

Downtown is two plumbing worlds in one. The condo towers along Front, Bay and the waterfront share vertical stacks, so an in-suite leak needs a stack shut-off, a notice to the property manager and a technician who knows the building rules. We do a lot of that, and we document each visit the way condo boards ask.

The Victorian and Edwardian rowhouses of Cabbagetown, Kensington, Trinity Bellwoods and the Annex are the other world: cast-iron stacks that have scaled for a hundred years, clay drains with roots, lead service lines from the street, and no access to the back of the lot. Camera first, then the smallest repair that lasts.

York

Our home base on Caledonia Rd

  • Backwater valves
  • Sump pumps
  • Basement floor drains
  • Water heaters
  • Galvanized repipes

Our shop is at 984 Caledonia Rd, so York, Fairbank, Oakwood Village, Weston and the Junction are the streets we drive most. The housing stock is postwar bungalows and semis with finished basements, which is exactly where sewer backups and failed sump pumps do the most damage.

This is where we install the most backwater valves in the city. If your basement has flooded in a July storm, or a neighbour's has, ask about the City subsidy that covers most of the cost.

North York

Split-levels, 1960s copper, Kitec in newer condos

  • Pinhole leaks
  • Shut-off valves
  • Kitec replacement
  • Water heaters
  • Main drain cleaning

Willowdale, Don Mills, Bayview Village and Downsview were built in waves from the 1950s to the 1970s, and the original copper and galvanized supply lines are now at the end of their lives. Pinhole leaks, seized gate valves and 40-year-old water heaters are the everyday calls here.

The condos and townhouses built around Yonge and Sheppard between 1995 and 2007 have a different problem: Kitec plastic plumbing, which many insurers now refuse to cover. We inspect for it and replace it.

East York

Clay drains, tree roots, 1920s to 1950s homes

  • Root cutting
  • Sewer line repair
  • Camera inspections
  • Clogged floor drains
  • Hose bibs

East York's small brick houses on deep, tree-lined lots in Leaside, Danforth Village, Woodbine Gardens and Thorncliffe are beautiful and hard on sewer lines. The laterals are clay, the trees are mature, and roots find every joint. Spring and fall are our busiest drain seasons here.

A camera inspection tells you whether an annual root cut is enough or whether a section needs replacing. Where it does, we try to save the walkway and the garden with a spot repair instead of a full dig.

Etobicoke

Long laterals, sump pumps, large lots

  • Sewer jetting
  • Sump pumps and backups
  • Water heaters
  • Burst pipes
  • Commercial plumbing

From Mimico and Long Branch to Islington, Kingsway and Rexdale, Etobicoke lots are bigger and sewer laterals are longer, so a blockage can be a long way from the house. Our jetting and camera equipment is built for that run.

Low-lying streets near the lake and the Humber rely on sump pumps. A pump without a battery backup is a flood waiting for a power outage; we add backups and test the whole system.

Scarborough

1960s to 1980s subdivisions, basement apartments

  • Drain cleaning
  • Water heater upgrades
  • Kitec replacement
  • Clogged sinks
  • Backwater valves

Agincourt, Malvern, Guildwood, Cliffside and West Hill were built for families and many homes now have a basement apartment, which doubles the load on the original drains and water heater. We size replacements for the house as it is used today.

Scarborough also has its share of Kitec in the townhouses of the late 1990s, and clay laterals in the older lakeside streets. We are in Scarborough daily; several of the before-and-after jobs on this site are from here.
