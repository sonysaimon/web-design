Signs you need this

  • Sink or tub drains slowly
  • Gurgling when the toilet flushes
  • Water at the basement floor drain
  • Sewage smell from a drain
  • More than one fixture backing up at once
  • Clogs that keep coming back

What we do

A clog in one fixture is usually hair, grease or scale in the trap or branch line. We cable it and test the flow. When more than one fixture backs up, or the basement floor drain is involved, the problem is in the main line, so we clear it and run a camera to see what caused it: roots in a clay lateral, a belly in the pipe, or a collapsed section.

  • Kitchen, bathroom and laundry sinks, tubs and showers

Why Toronto drains clog

Most houses in York, East York and the older parts of North York still drain through clay laterals laid before 1970. Tree roots find the joints, and every spring the line narrows a little more. Downtown, cast-iron stacks in century homes scale up from the inside. A camera inspection shows which one you have, so you can decide between an annual clean-out and a permanent fix.

Questions

How fast can you clear a drain?

Most single-fixture clogs are cleared on the first visit, usually the same day you book. Main-line backups are treated as urgent.

Do you use chemicals?

No. Liquid drain cleaners damage pipes and rarely fix the cause. We cable, jet and inspect.

Is a camera inspection worth it?

If the clog keeps coming back, or your home is older than 1970, yes. It tells you whether you are dealing with roots, a broken pipe or a one-off blockage, and the recording is useful for insurance claims and home sales.
