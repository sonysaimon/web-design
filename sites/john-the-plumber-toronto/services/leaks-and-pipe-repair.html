Leak detection and pipe repair in Toronto

Dripping, hissing, a stain on the ceiling or a water bill that jumped. Found and fixed, including the old galvanized and lead lines Toronto houses hide.

Signs you need this

  • Stain or bubble on a ceiling or wall
  • Water bill up with no change in use
  • Hissing or dripping inside a wall
  • Low pressure at one fixture
  • Valve that will not fully close
  • Pipe that froze last winter

What we do

Supply-line leaks, pinholes in old copper, failed shut-off valves, frozen and burst pipes, and the slow leaks under sinks and behind tubs that only show up as a stain. We repair the section with proper fittings, not tape and hope, and pressure-test before we leave.

Older Toronto homes

Houses built before the 1950s in Downtown, East York and York often still have a lead service line from the street, and galvanized steel inside that is rusting shut. If your water pressure has faded over the years, or the City has flagged your service as lead, we can replace the private side and coordinate with the City's replacement program for the public side.

Winter

Pipes on exterior walls and outdoor hose bibs are what freeze in Toronto. Before the first hard frost, shut off and drain outdoor faucets. If a pipe has frozen, leave the tap open a crack and call; thawing it the wrong way is how pipes burst.

Questions

Can you find a leak without tearing out the wall?

Usually. We trace it from the stain, the pressure drop and the pipe layout, then open the smallest section needed.

Is my main shut-off valve supposed to be stiff?

Old gate valves seize. If yours will not turn, replace it before you need it in an emergency. It is a quick job.

Do you replace lead pipes?

Yes, the private side from the property line into the house. We will tell you how the City's program works for the public side.
