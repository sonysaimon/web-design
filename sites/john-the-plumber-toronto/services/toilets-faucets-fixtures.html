Toilet, faucet and fixture repair in Toronto

Running toilet, dripping faucet, new vanity or shower set. Installed or repaired on one visit, with parts on the truck.

Book online (647) 697-0446

Signs you need this

  • Toilet runs, or needs a jiggle to stop
  • Toilet rocks or leaks at the base
  • Faucet drips or sprays
  • Low flow from one tap
  • New vanity, sink or shower set to install
  • Garbage disposal jammed or dead

What we do

  • Toilet repair and replacement, including seal and flange repairs
  • Kitchen, bathroom and laundry faucet repair and installation
  • Shower valves, tub spouts and touchless faucets
  • Vanity, sink and dishwasher hook-ups
  • Garbage disposals
  • Water softeners and filtration

Bring your own fixture or have us supply one. Either way we check the shut-off valves and supply lines while we are there, because a $12 braided hose is what usually floods a bathroom, not the faucet.

Pricing

Flat price per job, confirmed before the technician starts. Reviews mention this more than anything else: the price you hear first is the price you pay. First visit, $40 off.

Questions

Can I buy the fixture myself?

Yes. Tell us the model when you book so the technician brings the right fittings.

My toilet runs now and then. Is it urgent?

Not urgent, but it can waste hundreds of litres a day. It is usually a flapper or fill valve, a quick fix.

Do you install fixtures in condos?

Yes. We know the stack shut-off and property-manager notice rules downtown and will handle the paperwork.
