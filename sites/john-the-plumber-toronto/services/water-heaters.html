Water heater repair and installation in Toronto

No hot water, a leaking tank, or time to go tankless. Repairs the same day, replacements usually within 24 hours.

Book online (647) 697-0446

Signs you need this

  • No hot water, or it runs out fast
  • Water pooling under the tank
  • Rusty or smelly hot water
  • Pilot light keeps going out
  • Tank is over 10 years old
  • Rumbling or popping from the tank

Repair or replace

Elements, thermostats, gas valves and anode rods can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot. If your heater is under eight years old we usually repair it; past twelve, a replacement is the better money. The technician tells you which before any work starts.

  • Gas and electric tank water heaters
  • Tankless (on-demand) installation and annual descaling
  • Power-vent and direct-vent units
  • Owned units to replace a rental tank
  • Expansion tanks and pressure-reducing valves

Tankless in Toronto

Toronto's winter inlet water arrives at about 4 °C, so a tankless unit has to be sized for that, not for the brochure number. We size it to your fixtures and gas line, and because the city's water is moderately hard, we set up a yearly descale so it keeps its flow rate.

Questions

How long does a replacement take?

Usually two to three hours on site once the unit is here. Standard tanks are often same day or next morning.

I rent my tank. Can you still help?

If it is a rental, the provider is responsible for it. Many customers switch to an owned unit when the rental fails; we can walk you through the buy-out.

Is tankless worth it?

For a household that runs out of hot water, or wants the floor space back, yes. For a small home with low use, a good tank is cheaper. We will say which applies to you.
