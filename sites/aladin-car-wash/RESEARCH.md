## Design plan (Phase 3)
Subject: a 25-year-old Burnaby tunnel car wash under a new, hands-on owner (Shahryar) who personally details cars. Audience: Burnaby Heights / North Burnaby drivers, dog owners, people with a neglected interior. Page job: get a detail booked (Calendly) or a call; make the drive-in washes and $49.99 memberships obvious.
Palette: --ink #060B16 (page), --navy #0B1730 (surfaces), --card #111F3D, --blue #2F7CFF (tunnel light, primary), --pink #FF4FA0 (brush pink, used ONLY for the Supreme tier mark and one hairline), --foam #F3F6FC (text), --mist #9DAAC4 (muted), --line rgba(159,170,196,.16)
Type: display "Bricolage Grotesque" 800 (wide, optical sizes, characterful, not the usual Archivo/Inter), body "Instrument Sans" 400/500/600, numerals tabular. H1 clamp(44px, 7.4vw, 108px), tight leading -0.03em.
Signature: the TUNNEL ARCH — the hero photo (blue-lit tunnel) sits inside a tall arch-shaped mask (round-top, flat bottom) at the right of the hero, parallaxing behind it; the same arch mask is reused once for the owner photo. Section eyebrows carry a tiny brush-stripe (3 blue / 1 pink bars) that recalls the brush colours in the tunnel.
Interactive on first screen: hold-to-compare Before/After card (Ram 1500 interior, owner's own photo) captioned "Ram 1500 · full interior detail".
Structure (single page): nav (Washes · Memberships · Detailing · Reviews · Visit + phone button + Book a detail) → hero (eyebrow "North Burnaby · 6123 Hastings St · next to Tim Hortons", H1 "Your car deserves the royal treatment." (their line), facts line, two CTAs, before/after card) → trust strip (4.4★ 398 Google reviews · Since 1999 · Buy 3 get 1 free · 70% touch-free · 7 days) → Washes (3 tiers, single/monthly toggle, "just drive in") → Detailing (4 cards incl. ozone w/ prices; Book → Calendly) → Weekend pickup (parallax band + 4 steps) → Self-serve bays → The work (named cards) → Shahryar story (masked photo) → Reviews → FAQ → Visit (hours, map, contact) → footer. Sticky mobile bar: Call / Book.
Self-critique vs generic defaults: not cream+serif, not black+acid green; dark navy is the brand's actual blue world, the arch and brush stripe come from the tunnel itself. Risk taken: pink as a one-place accent on a navy site. Broadway Locksmith (earlier demo) was navy+brass with serif; this is blue-black + electric blue + grotesque, no serif, different structure.
