FAQ

Full home questions

What does a full home renovation cost?

Typically $200 to $350 per square foot in Vancouver, depending on scope, finishes, structural changes and permits. The fixed quote breaks that down line by line.

How long does it take?

Most full home renovations take 6 to 16 weeks depending on size and complexity. The timeline is set in your quote and tracked with daily updates.

Do you build basement suites and laneway houses?

Yes. Basement conversions, secondary suites, additions and laneway or coach houses are designed and built to local zoning, including building approval.

What about asbestos in older homes?

Older homes can contain asbestos and lead-based paint. We test before demolition and arrange safe, compliant removal.

Can we live in the house during the work?

It depends on scope. Phased work is possible for some projects; we tell you honestly at the consultation whether moving out for part of the build makes sense.
