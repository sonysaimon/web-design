Condo work is as much about the building as the unit. Grand Renovations prepares and submits strata alteration agreements, books elevators for deliveries and debris, schedules work inside permitted noise hours, protects hallways and elevators, carries insurance naming the strata corporation and installs flooring that meets STC/IIC acoustic ratings. FAQ

Condos questions

Do you deal with my strata?

Yes. We prepare and submit the alteration agreement, provide the insurance documentation your strata asks for and coordinate scheduling with building management.

What hours can you work in my building?

Inside your building's permitted noise hours. Noisy work such as demolition and tile cutting is scheduled accordingly.

Do condo renovations need a City permit?

Plumbing and electrical changes need a City of Vancouver permit in addition to strata approval. We handle both.

How long does a condo renovation take?

Most projects take several weeks once construction begins; a single bathroom or kitchen is shorter than a whole-unit renovation. Your fixed quote includes the schedule.

Which areas do you cover for condos?

Vancouver, Yaletown, the West End, Kitsilano, North Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond and Surrey.
