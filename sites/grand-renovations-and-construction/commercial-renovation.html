How it works

Five steps, no surprises

  1. 1

    Free consultation

    We meet at your home, walk the space, listen to what you want and flag any structural or permit questions up front. No obligation.

  2. 2

    Fixed quote + design

    You get a line-by-line fixed-price quote with 3D renderings, material selections and a clear timeline before anything starts.

  3. 3

    Permits + strata

    We file City of Vancouver permits and strata alteration applications, book elevators and handle the paperwork for you.

  4. 4

    Build

    A dedicated site supervisor runs every trade, tile, plumbing, electrical and millwork, with daily progress updates and a clean site.

  5. 5

    Walkthrough + warranty

    We walk the finished job together, fix any finishing details and hand over your written workmanship warranty.

FAQ

Commercial questions

What commercial work do you take on?

Tenant improvements and full renovations for hotels, restaurants, commercial kitchens, offices, dental clinics and similar spaces.

Can you work around an operating business?

Yes. Hospitality projects are phased and scheduled so the business keeps running where possible; the schedule is agreed in the quote.

Who handles permits and engineering?

We do. City permits, architectural and engineering coordination are part of our project management.

Are you insured for commercial sites?

Grand Renovations carries $5M liability insurance and WorkSafeBC coverage, and can provide documentation naming your landlord or strata.
