FAQ

Basements questions

Do you handle the permits for a suite?

Yes. Secondary suites, additions and laneway houses all need City of Vancouver approval; we manage the application and coordinate any engineering.

How long does a basement take?

It depends on whether a full suite with a kitchen and bathroom is included. The schedule is fixed in your quote.

Can you add a bathroom in the basement?

Yes. Plumbing rough-ins, waterproofing and ventilation are all part of our bathroom work.

What about moisture in Vancouver basements?

Insulation, vapour control and proper ventilation are planned before finishes go in; we tell you at the consultation if drainage work is needed first.
