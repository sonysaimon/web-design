How it works

Five steps, no surprises

The same workflow on a condo bathroom or a whole-home rebuild.

  1. 1

    Free consultation

    We meet at your home, walk the space, listen to what you want and flag any structural or permit questions up front. No obligation.

  2. 2

    Fixed quote + design

    You get a line-by-line fixed-price quote with 3D renderings, material selections and a clear timeline before anything starts.

  3. 3

    Permits + strata

    We file City of Vancouver permits and strata alteration applications, book elevators and handle the paperwork for you.

  4. 4

    Build

    A dedicated site supervisor runs every trade, tile, plumbing, electrical and millwork, with daily progress updates and a clean site.

  5. 5

    Walkthrough + warranty

    We walk the finished job together, fix any finishing details and hand over your written workmanship warranty.

FAQ

Questions homeowners ask first

How do I get a quote?

Call (604) 990-2747 or send the short form below. We meet at your home for a free consultation, then you receive a line-by-line fixed-price quote with 3D renderings and a timeline.

How long will my renovation take?

Bathrooms usually take 2 to 6 weeks, kitchens 4 to 12 weeks and full home renovations 6 to 16 weeks. Your quote fixes the schedule for your project.

Do you handle permits and strata approvals?

Yes. City of Vancouver permits, strata alteration agreements, elevator bookings and building coordination are all managed by us.

Do you do the design too?

Yes. Grand Renovations is design-build: our designers and project managers produce the plan and 3D renderings, then the same team builds it.

Is there a warranty?

Every project includes a written workmanship warranty, handed over at the final walkthrough, plus help with manufacturer warranties on products.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes: City of Vancouver business licence #25-104757, $5M liability insurance, WorkSafeBC account #969861, BBB A+ accredited and an NKBA member.
