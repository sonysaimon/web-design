FAQ

Kitchens questions

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

Most kitchens take 4 to 12 weeks of construction depending on scope. Cabinetry lead time is planned before demolition so the kitchen is not out of action longer than needed.

What does a kitchen renovation cost in Vancouver?

Smaller kitchens start around $15,000; full renovations begin around $25,000, and the Vancouver average sits between $40,000 and $45,000. Custom kitchens with structural work run $70,000 to $150,000+. You get a fixed, line-by-line quote before work starts.

Do you handle the permit?

Yes. Moving walls, relocating plumbing or adding circuits needs a City of Vancouver permit, and Grand Renovations files and manages it.

Can you redesign a condo galley kitchen?

Yes. Several recent reviews are for North Vancouver galley kitchens redesigned for better flow, and condo work includes the strata approvals.

Is there a warranty?

Every project comes with a written workmanship warranty, and we help with manufacturer warranties on cabinets, counters and appliances.
