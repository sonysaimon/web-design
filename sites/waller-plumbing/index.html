While we're on the way

  1. Shut off the main water valve. It's usually where the line enters the house, near the meter or the hot water tank.
  2. Leaking hot water tank? Turn its gas valve to Pilot or switch off its breaker, then close the cold inlet on top.
  3. Smell gas? Get everyone outside first, then call FortisBC at 1-800-663-9911, then us.
  4. Move what you can off the floor. Towels at doorways buy time.

Counts as an emergency: burst or frozen pipes · sewage coming up a drain · flooded basement · no water · leaking tank · gas smell.

Questions

Are you really open 24 hours?

Yes. The same number, 778 898 6189, rings Adam day and night, seven days a week. Nights and weekends are when most hot water tanks and water mains decide to go.

You get a price before any work starts, and that's the price you pay. No separate charge just to come out and look.

Yes. Waller Plumbing is a Technical Safety BC licensed gas contractor, licence LGA0208910, so hot water tanks, furnaces and gas lines are done with the proper permit.
