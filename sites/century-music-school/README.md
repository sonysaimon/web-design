# Vancouver Century Music School — demo website

Website for Vancouver Century Music School, 1730 Kingsway, Vancouver. Built 2 Nov 2026 by Sony Saimon
as a demo to pitch to the owner. Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. It carries every piece of
information from centurymusic.ca: programs, prices, trial terms, the 13 teacher bios with photos,
RCM results, testimonials, the full rescheduling and school policies, the IELTS/CELPIP/TOEFL
course, all 45 blog posts, and a Chinese version.

## Pages
- `index.html` — home: hero, credentials, what we offer, 9 programs, trial + tuition, RCM results, teachers, reviews, visit (map, QR codes, socials), FAQ, latest articles.
- `teachers.html` — all 13 teachers with photos and full bios (rendered from `services.js`).
- `policies.html` — rescheduling policy and options, policy agreement, booking and renewal, trial terms, make-ups, refunds, expiry, scheduling, extra fees, facility rules, holidays, group rates, payment FAQ.
- `english.html` — IELTS/CELPIP/TOEFL 28-hour course and VIP one-to-one, schedule, how online classes work.
- `blog.html` and `blog/<slug>.html` — 45 articles, generated by `build_blog.py` from `blog/posts.json`.
- `zh.html` — 中文 version of the home page, including the school's own Chinese text for the English course and the remote-learning FAQ.

## Files
- `styles.css` — tokens in `:root` (green, ivory, gold), sections, sub-pages, responsive.
- `services.js` — ALL editable content for the English pages: programs, trial, tuition (prices exactly as on centurymusic.ca), teachers + bios, RCM results, reviews, grouped FAQ, links.
- `main.js` — renders `services.js` on whichever page it runs, mobile menu, open/closed status (Mon 3–7, Tue–Fri 3–7:30, Sat 8:30–6).
- `build_blog.py` — rebuilds the blog pages: `python3 build_blog.py`. Add a post to `blog/posts.json` (slug, title, date, html) and run it.
- `photos/` — the school's own photos: storefronts, lesson, keys, students with medals, recital, WhatsApp/WeChat QR codes, award badges (Best of Vancouver 2023, Three Best Rated 2019–2025, RCM Teachers of Distinction 2025, RCM certified teacher, BCRMTA member), the instrument banners, and `photos/teachers/` headshots.
- `favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `deploy.sh`.

## Edit prices, teachers or FAQ
Open `services.js`. `tuition` holds the packages, `trial` the $158 options, `teachers` the faculty (photo file, credentials, bio paragraphs), `results` the RCM list, `faq` the questions. The Chinese page is plain HTML in `zh.html`; change it by hand to match.

## Book buttons
"Book a trial" links to the school's existing trial purchase page on centurymusic.ca; "Text" opens an SMS to 778-723-9999. Change `trialUrl` in `services.js` (and the header/mobile-bar links) if they move the store.
