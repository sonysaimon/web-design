Rescheduling policy

Before requesting a reschedule, please review the deadlines below. Then submit your request by text (778‑723‑9999) or email, choosing one option only. Include:

  • Student's full name
  • Instructor's name
  • Dates that will be missed
  • As many alternative dates and times as possible for a make‑up class

Make‑up classes cannot be scheduled during your regular lesson time. Rescheduling with the same instructor is subject to availability. Requests submitted after the deadlines below, or last‑minute notice, will not receive a response.

Rescheduling options

Standard reschedule: 24 hours' notice

  • Must be requested at least 24 hours in advance
  • Make‑up must be scheduled within 2 weeks, in person or via Zoom

Postponement: 48 hours' notice

  • Must be requested at least 48 hours in advance
  • The class may be postponed to after the end of the current term
  • Limit of 2 postponements per 4‑month period

Emergency request

  • Must be received before 10:00 am for a weekday lesson, or before 10:00 pm the night before for a weekend lesson
  • The make‑up must be completed within the same week via Zoom; if not, the class is forfeited

Late emergency (missed deadline)

If an emergency request is submitted past the deadline, the class is considered missed with no make‑up.

Summer rescheduling (July and August only)

24‑hour advance notice is required for any postponement during this period.

Lesson request confirmation and school policy agreement

By submitting a request, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

  • Lessons are scheduled based on enrollment, not attendance. Extended or long‑term absences may result in loss of the current lesson spot, and the school reserves the right to cancel lessons.
  • Make‑up lessons are a courtesy, not a guarantee, and are subject to teacher and time availability.
  • Make‑up lessons must be scheduled outside of the student's regular lesson time, may be conducted by a different instructor, and must be completed within 2 weeks of the original lesson date.
  • Once rescheduled, a lesson cannot be rescheduled again.
  • A make‑up is not confirmed until you receive confirmation from CMS staff.
  • Lateness, no‑shows or forgetting to attend a class are not eligible for a make‑up or notification.
  • Make‑ups apply only to private lessons and are not available for group classes.
  • There are no classes on Christmas Day (December 25) and New Year's Day (January 1). Lessons proceed as usual on all other statutory holidays. You must notify the school in advance if you plan to be absent on a statutory holiday.

Booking and renewal guidelines

To book or renew lessons, visit the payment page and complete your order at least one day in advance. Payment must be made by e‑Transfer (EMT) or online transaction to secure your lesson time with a teacher. All lessons are expected to be attended weekly unless otherwise arranged. Lessons are booked once or twice a week, continuously.

Trial classes

  • Available to new students only, limited to one person per instrument and one trial package per family
  • Appointment required; non‑refundable; cannot be combined with other promotions
  • Must be booked within two weeks, otherwise the offer expires; the package is valid for one month from the issue date if no booking activity occurs (the online store lists two months, so confirm with the school)
  • A 48‑hour notice is required for cancellation, postponement or a one‑time rescheduling
  • Lessons must be scheduled at least once per week

Make‑up lessons

  • Applicable to private lessons only, not group classes
  • Make‑up lessons cannot be rescheduled again

Refunds and class switching

  • Refunds are not available after the first lesson has started.
  • Students may switch to a different class of equal duration if needed. For 1:1 classes, let us know one week in advance which class you'd like to try or switch to.

Lesson expiry

Paid lessons expire after six months of non‑attendance.

Class scheduling

Class time slots are filled on a first‑come, first‑paid basis. To secure your existing preferred time slot, renew at least one week before the start of the new term.

Additional fees

Lesson fees do not include:

  • Exam fees
  • School room rental for exams (charged as one regular lesson)
  • Books, materials or recital fees

Facility rules

For the comfort and safety of all students, the following are prohibited in the facility:

  • Food or drinks
  • Allergy‑inducing substances
  • Perfume or scented products
  • Loud noises
  • Smoking

Parents and guardians are welcome to drop off and pick up, sit in to watch a class, or wait outside the classroom.

Holiday schedule

  • No classes on Christmas Day (December 25) and New Year's Day (January 1)
  • Classes run on all other statutory holidays
  • Please give advance notice if you plan to miss a class for a holiday; without notice, the lesson is charged as usual

Group class rates

Form a group with your friends:

Small group class (3–4 students, 45 minutes)$35 per student
Semi‑private class (2 students, 45 minutes)$40 per student

Ensembles (duets such as piano and violin, two violins, guitar and violin, guitar and piano) are for students taking private lessons concurrently or at intermediate level. No make‑up classes are available for group classes.

Payment FAQ

How do I register or book a class?

On the payment page, choose and purchase the trial class for new students or select a package of 30‑, 45‑ or 60‑minute lessons. Upon payment, please indicate which class you would like to take and the student's name for faster booking. EMT is also accepted.

What is the payment method?

Fees can be paid directly online on the website, or through Interac e‑Transfer with additional discounts for larger packages. Once received, we confirm the lesson time to you.

Is there an age requirement?

For piano, students may start as young as 4, up to adults and seniors. For all other music, arts and language classes, we recommend age 5 and above.

Is a parent or guardian required to stay?

There is no requirement. A parent or guardian may drop off and pick up the student when class is finished, sit in to watch, or rest outside the classroom.

What happens if the student can't come to a class?

See the rescheduling policy above. For any other reason without notice, the class is charged without a make‑up lesson.
