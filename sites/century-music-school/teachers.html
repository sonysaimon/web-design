Faculty

Twelve music teachers and an English teacher.

Our instructors are certified by the Royal Conservatory of Music, the BC Registered Music Teachers' Association (BCRMTA), the Canadian Federation of Music Teachers' Associations (CFMTA) and the North American Musicians Association. Many also hold Master's degrees in music and education. Our goal is to foster confidence, self‑esteem and a lifelong love of music in every student.
