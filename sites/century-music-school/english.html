How online classes work

After you order online you receive an email receipt. Download Zoom. On the day of class we email you the link, date and time. About 30 minutes before the class, click the link; the teacher's video page opens, and the teacher checks that your microphone and camera work before starting. When the class ends, simply close the video page.

A camera is not required. If your microphone doesn't work, you can type to communicate with the teacher. Free online class materials and mock exam papers are provided to every student in the course.
