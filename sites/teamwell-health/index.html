New patients

Your first visit, in plain words.

  1. 1

    Ask for a time

    Send the request form below or call. Say who you'd like to see and whether after-work suits you better. We confirm by phone or email.

  2. 2

    Talk first

    What hurts, when it started, what you've tried. Then a physical assessment. Allow extra time for a first visit; naturopathic first visits run longer.

  3. 3

    Hear the plan

    What's going on, in plain language, and what the plan is. Treatment usually starts the same visit if it's appropriate.

Bring with you

  • Your extended-health plan details
  • ICBC claim number and crash date, for car-accident visits
  • Any imaging or reports you already have
  • Clothes you can move in

Why TeamWell

One plan, not three opinions.

Most people with a bad back, a crash injury or a year of low energy end up carrying their own story between offices. Here the chiropractor, the naturopathic doctor and the acupuncturist work down the same hallway, so when your care needs more than one of them, the handoff is a conversation, not a referral letter.
