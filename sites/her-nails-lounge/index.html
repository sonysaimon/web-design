The lounge

Crafted to catch the light.

Warm oak, rattan and plaster, a wall of polish, and a team of industry professionals with years of experience. We keep the room quiet so the work can be precise.

Every service starts with careful dry cuticle prep and finishes in gel formulas chosen for how they wear, not just how they look on day one. Even the simplest set gets our full attention.

Gel‑specialized
Builder gel, hard gel, Gel‑X and dual‑form extensions, all cured to last.
Non‑toxic formulas
Low‑odour, HEMA‑conscious products that are kinder to your natural nail.
Dry manicure
Russian e‑file technique for an ultra‑clean cuticle line and longer‑lasting gel.

Before you book

  • Wearing builder gel, hard gel, acrylic or extensions? Add a Removal from the add‑ons so we can reserve the time.
  • Want nail art? French, cat eye, ombre and chrome all count. Add them at booking so your set is never rushed.
  • Not sure what to pick? Send your inspo photos to @hernailslounge and we’ll point you to the right booking.
