About Plumbers Direct

Twenty-five years. One phone number.

Plumbers Direct has been fixing Toronto plumbing since 2000: residential and commercial, routine upkeep to the 4 a.m. burst pipe. We are a small, women-owned company, and customers know us by the people who answer and show up.

What we do: faucets and toilets, drains and sewers, leaking and frozen pipes, sump pumps and backwater valves, water mains, hot water tanks, water quality, and bathroom and kitchen plumbing for renovations. We pull permits, close them, and give you a written quote before any work begins.
