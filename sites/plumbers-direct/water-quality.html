Water quality

Hard water leaves scale on fixtures, shortens appliance life and makes soap useless. A softener or filter fixes it at the source.

Call 416-402-7798Request service

Toronto's municipal water is moderately hard. Over years that means white scale on faucets and kettles, cloudy glassware, and heating elements that fail early. A softener installed at the main treats the whole house.

For taste, odour or specific concerns we install NSF-certified point-of-use and whole-home filtration systems, and we can test your water first so you buy the right system rather than the biggest one.

We have installed complicated filtration setups for homeowners who needed them and told others that a simple under-sink filter would do. We quote what fits.

Questions

Do I need a softener in Toronto?
Not everyone does. If you see scale build-up, dry skin after showers or shortened appliance life, it is worth a conversation.
How often do filters need changing?
Depends on the system: six to twelve months for most cartridges. We note it on the invoice.
Is Toronto tap water safe?
Yes. Filtration is about taste, hardness and specific household preferences, not safety.
