    Toronto homeowners

    The City will pay up to $6,650 toward flood protection.

    The City of Toronto Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program pays up to 80% of the invoiced cost of a backwater valve, a sump pump with battery back-up and related work when a licensed plumber installs it with a permit. Expanded on May 1, 2026. We do the installation and give you the invoice and licence details the application needs.

    • Backwater valve: up to $1,600 per device, maximum two
    • Sump pump with battery back-up: up to $2,250
    • Plus pipe severance and capping, and a home plumbing assessment

    Questions

    Before you call.

    How much does a plumber cost in Toronto?
    It depends on the job, so we price it on site, in writing, before any work starts. Reviews describe "a price upfront, no hidden fees" and quotes honoured even when the job grew. For a plumbing emergency call and ask; you will get a straight answer.
    How fast can you get here?
    Same day for almost everything, and often within the hour for emergencies in the west end. Joyce will tell you the real time when you call rather than a promise we cannot keep.
    Do you charge more at night or on weekends?
    The emergency number is the same 24 hours a day. Customers have written that they were not charged an after-hours surcharge for a Sunday-night call. Ask when you call.
    Are you licensed and insured?
    Yes. City of Toronto plumbing licence P26432. We pull permits where the work requires them, which also matters if you are applying for the City's flood-protection subsidy.
    What is covered by the warranty?
    All our work, for one full year. And if we can't fix the problem, you don't pay for the attempt.
    Do you do commercial work?
    Yes: small commercial, retail, salons, property managers and permit close-outs. Several of our longest-running customers manage buildings and call us for all of them.
