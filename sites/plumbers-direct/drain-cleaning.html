Drain cleaning & replacement

Snaked the same day. If the camera shows a broken pipe, you get the footage and a written price before anything is dug.

Call 416-402-7798Request service

Most clogs are grease, hair, soap scum or coffee grounds and clear with a proper auger. We clear the line, run water to confirm flow, and tell you what caused it so it does not come back next month.

If a drain backs up again and again, or more than one fixture is slow, the problem is further down the line. A camera inspection shows exactly what is there: a belly in the pipe, a tree root, a collapsed clay section. You see the footage with us.

When a section has failed we replace only what needs replacing, pull the permit where the City requires one, and restore the floor or yard. Drain replacement work is covered by our one-year warranty like everything else.

What customers said about this work

Questions

How fast can you clear a drain?
Usually the same day. Reviews mention a plumber at the door within an hour for emergencies.
Do I need a camera inspection?
Not for a first-time clog. We recommend it when a drain backs up repeatedly, after a sewer back-up, or before you buy a house with older clay drains.
Will chemical drain cleaner work?
It can soften a grease clog but it damages older pipes and does nothing for roots or collapsed sections. If you have already used it, tell us before we open the line.
