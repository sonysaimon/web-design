Based in Bloor West. Across the GTA.

Toronto west end

High Park, Bloor West Village, The Junction, Roncesvalles, Swansea, Parkdale. Our home base, minutes away. Houses here are 1900s–1940s: clay drains, lead water services and galvanized supply lines are the everyday calls.

Downtown & Midtown Toronto

Condos, walk-ups and Victorian semis. Condo work needs the building's shut-off coordinated with the property manager; we do that regularly.

Etobicoke

Post-war bungalows and 1960s–70s subdivisions. Sump pumps, backwater valves and basement flooding after summer storms.

North York, York & East York

Mixed housing stock. Water heater replacements, drain clogs and sewer back-ups on older streets.

Leslieville, The Beach & Scarborough

East-end homes, many with original drains. Camera inspections and backwater valve installs are common here.

Mississauga & Oakville

West of the city. Same-day service for most calls; emergencies dispatched from the west end.

Vaughan, Woodbridge, Thornhill & Richmond Hill

North of the 401. Newer homes with polybutylene supply lines from the 80s and 90s show up here.

Brampton & Markham

Outer GTA. We come for scheduled work and emergencies; call for a realistic time.
