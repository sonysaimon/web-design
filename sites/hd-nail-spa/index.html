Good to know

Before you come in.

Where do I park?
Free parking behind the salon. Enter from the lane off West Broadway. Street parking on Broadway is metered.
How do I book?
Online on Fresha with instant confirmation, and you can pay in the app. Pick a technician by name or take the first available. Or call (604) 731‑4599.
Is removal extra?
Gel manicures and pedicures have a version with gel removal included ($5 more). Taking off acrylic, Gel-X or hard gel is $10 as an add-on to another service, or $21 on its own. BIAB removal is $5 as an add-on.
Do you do gift cards?
Yes, through Fresha. Buy online and it's delivered by email, for any service or amount.
Can I bring my kids?
There's a Little Ones menu for children under 10, from $11 for a polish change to $41 for a gel pedicure.
What about lashes and waxing?
Keratin lash lifts, classic through mega volume extensions, tinting, and waxing from eyebrows to full legs. Book them alone or with a nail service.
