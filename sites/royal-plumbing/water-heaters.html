Toronto specifics

What's normal in a Toronto basement

Toronto water is moderately hard, so tanks here typically last 10 to 15 years before sediment and corrosion catch up. If yours is older than 12 and you see rust at the base or hear it rumble, replacing it before it fails saves a flooded basement.

A lot of the city's heaters are rentals from the utility era. We'll explain the buy-out math honestly: sometimes it's worth it, sometimes the rental is fine for a few more years.

Tankless makes sense in a busy family house with a good gas supply and somewhere to vent. In a small semi with one bathroom, a well-sized conventional tank is usually the smarter money, and we'll say so.

Questions

Good to know

How long does a replacement take?

About two to three hours on site once the new tank is on the truck. Call before noon and it's usually done the same day.

Should I repair or replace?

Under 8 years old with a single failed part: repair. Over 12 years, leaking from the tank body, or a second major repair: replace. We give you both prices and let you choose.

Do you take the old tank away?

Yes, it leaves with us and is recycled.

Tank or tankless?

Tankless costs more up front and needs the right gas supply and venting. In a busy household it pays back in bills and never runs out. We'll tell you if your house isn't a good fit.
