Basement flooding protection

Since May 2026 the City pays up to 80% of the cost, to a maximum of $6,650 per property: up to $1,600 per backwater valve (two allowed), $2,250 for a sump pump, $300 for its battery back-up, $400 to sever the foundation drain and $500 for a plumbing assessment. We install it, document it and help you file.

Eligibility and amounts set by the City of Toronto Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy Program; work must be done by a licensed plumber.

Toronto know-how

Old houses, old pipes, no surprises

Twenty-plus years in the west end means we've seen inside most of the housing stock from Parkdale to Leaside. What we find most often:

  • Clay sewer laterals in pre-1960 homes let tree roots in at every joint. Camera first, then clear, line or replace.
  • Cast-iron stacks rust through from the inside after 60 to 80 years. The photo shows one we replaced in a Dufferin Grove semi.
  • Galvanized supply lines choke down to a pencil's width. Low pressure and brown water mean it's time for copper or PEX.
  • Combined sewers downtown back up in storms. A backwater valve plus a sump pump, with the City's subsidy, is the fix.
  • Condo stacks and shut-offs tucked behind dryers and vanities. We find them, and we tell you where they are for next time.

Questions

Before you call

Do you charge for estimates or a trip fee?

No. Estimates are free, there is no trip fee, and you get the full price in writing before any work starts. You decide whether to go ahead.

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. The office answers Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Outside those hours the same number reaches the on-call service and the plumber on duty calls you back, usually within minutes. Ask about after-hours rates when you call.

How fast can a plumber get here?

Plumbers are dispatched from the location nearest you, not just Dufferin Street. For emergencies, customers regularly report a callback within five to ten minutes and a plumber at the door in 30 to 60 minutes. Most non-urgent jobs are booked the same or next day.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Royal Plumbing is a licensed and insured plumbing contractor in Toronto and has been BBB A+ Accredited for years. Every plumber we send is a licensed professional, and our gas work is done by licensed gas fitters.

Is the work guaranteed?

All workmanship is backed by a written guarantee. If something we did isn't right, we come back and make it right. Fixtures and equipment carry the manufacturer's warranty on top.

Is there a rebate for a backwater valve or sump pump?

Yes. The City of Toronto Basement Flooding Protection Subsidy covers up to 80% of the cost, to a maximum of $6,650 per property, for backwater valves, sump pumps and battery back-ups, foundation drain severance and a plumbing assessment, when a licensed plumber does the work. We handle the paperwork with you.

Which areas do you cover?

All of the City of Toronto plus Peel, York, Halton and Durham regions. See the service areas page for the full list, and call if you're just outside it.
