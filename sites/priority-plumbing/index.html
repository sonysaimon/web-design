About

We don’t dabble in residential service

Austin Tsakopoulos started Priority in 2001 because the plumbers who were great on a construction site were showing up in people’s homes smelling of smoke and hiding mistakes behind drywall.

Twenty-four years later the company only does residential service: plumbing, drains, sewer, radiator heating and waterproofing. Technicians are hired for critical thinking and manners first, then trained on the brands and tools in your house.

Questions

Before you book

How fast can you come?

Same day for most calls, and emergencies are prioritized by dispatch even on full days. The emergency line is answered 24 hours.

Do you charge more at night or on weekends?

No. One rate, seven days a week.

How does pricing work?

The technician diagnoses first, then gives you a firm price with options before any work starts. No hourly meter. Ask about the $99 main drain clearing and the $50 off any plumbing service.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Plumbing licence #P17794, heating licence #00749274, fully insured, and our technicians are background-checked employees, not subcontractors.

What’s covered by the warranty?

Every installation carries a lifetime warranty, and all workmanship is backed by our make-it-right-or-you-don’t-pay guarantee.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Toronto plus Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Thornhill, Maple, Concord and Oakville. See service areas.
