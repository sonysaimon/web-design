    Questions

    Good to know before you call

    Do you really answer at night and on weekends?

    Yes. The line is staffed 24 hours, 7 days. Burst pipes, sewer backups and basement flooding go to the front of the queue.

    How fast can someone be here?

    For emergencies in Toronto we aim to be on site within the hour. Most non-urgent calls are booked the same day.

    How does pricing work?

    We diagnose first, then give you the price before any work starts. No hidden fees and no upsell. The same rate applies nights and weekends.

    Is the work guaranteed?

    Yes. Labour and the parts we supply are covered by our warranty. If something we fixed gives you trouble, we come back and make it right at no extra cost.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes. Our plumbers are licensed in Ontario and the company carries liability insurance. Ask for the licence number when you book and we'll give it to you.

    Where do you work?

    We are based on Davenport Rd near Dufferin and cover all of Toronto, plus Vaughan, Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Richmond Hill, Pickering, Oshawa and Burlington.

    Do you do renovations, or just repairs?

    Both. Full bathroom and kitchen plumbing for renovations, new fixtures, vanities, shower drains and rough-ins, as well as repairs and emergencies.
