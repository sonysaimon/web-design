Questions

Water heaters FAQ

How long does a water heater last?

A tank typically lasts 10 to 12 years in Toronto water; tankless units longer with yearly service. Past that, leaks are a matter of time.

Tank or tankless?

Tankless saves space and gives endless hot water, but costs more up front and needs the right gas supply. For many houses a good tank is still the sensible choice. We'll be honest about which fits.

Can you come today?

Usually, yes. No hot water is treated as a same-day call.
