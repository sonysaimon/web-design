How it goes

What happens when you call

Find the blockage

We work out which part of the system is affected and what's causing it: grease, hair, wipes, roots or a sagging pipe.

Clear the full line

Augers and snakes for most clogs; hydro-jetting when there's buildup along the pipe walls. No harsh chemicals.

Camera, flush and test

For stubborn or repeat clogs we run a camera to see the pipe itself, then flush and test every fixture before we leave.

Questions

Drain cleaning FAQ

Snake or hydro-jet, which do I need?

A snake clears the blockage. A hydro-jet scours the whole pipe clean and is the better choice when a drain clogs repeatedly or you have grease and root buildup. We'll tell you which one your line needs and why.

Can I just use a chemical drain cleaner?

We'd rather you didn't. They rarely clear more than the surface of a clog and they corrode older pipes, which turns a cheap fix into an expensive one.

Do you clean drains at night?

Yes. Same crew, same rate, 24 hours.
