Getting here

Easy by seawall, bike or bus

Walking & the seawall

We're right on the False Creek seawall at the foot of Beach Avenue, under the Burrard Street Bridge — a five-minute stroll from Sunset Beach and the Aquatic Centre, ten from English Bay Beach.

By bike

The Beach Avenue protected bike lane and the seawall cycling path both run past the door. Bike racks are nearby along the seawall.

Parking & transit

Metered street parking on Beach Avenue and Pacific Street, plus the Vancouver Aquatic Centre lot a block away. The #23 Beach bus stops nearby; the Aquatic Centre False Creek Ferries dock is a short walk.

Good to know

FAQ

Do you take reservations?

Yes — by phone. Call (604) 676-2337 and we'll set you up. Walk-ins are always welcome, and the patio is first-come on sunny days, so arrive early for sunset.

When is happy hour?

Monday to Friday, 3–5pm: $5.50 select pints, $7.50 glasses of wine, and snacks from $7.50. The full list is on the happy hour menu.

When is brunch?

Saturday and Sunday, 10am–2pm — five kinds of eggs benny, chicken 'n waffles, breakfast burgers and more. See the brunch menu.

Can you host groups?

Yes — we're happy to accommodate larger tables (one review raves about us seating a party of seven on a Saturday night). Call ahead for groups; parties of 8 or more may be subject to an 18% gratuity.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely — we have great options for kids and the kitchen is happy to adapt dishes.

Do you have vegetarian and gluten-free options?

Yes — the Beyond Beach Burger, avocado tostadas, salads and more, plus gluten-free buns for any handheld ($2.50).

Do you offer takeout?

Yes, dine-in and takeout. Call (604) 676-2337 to order ahead.
